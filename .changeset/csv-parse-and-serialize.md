Add `javascript-excel/csv`: `parseCsv`, `readCsv`, `toCsv` and `toCsvBlob`. RFC 4180 parsing with delimiter detection, BOM and CRLF/LF/CR handling, lenient or strict modes, optional type inference, and an async `readCsv` with encoding detection and AbortSignal support. Serialization with quoting, BOM, ISO dates and a formula-injection guard that is on by default.
